Slow-Mo Aim: What does the aim indicator look like?

While right-click is held, time slows and an aim indicator appears. Which style fits best?

SLOW

A — Crosshair Only

Simple crosshair cursor replaces the default pointer. Clean, minimal. No trajectory preview — player must judge the arc themselves. Screen gets a subtle slow-mo tint.

SLOW

B — Dotted Trajectory Arc

Dotted arc preview shows the shuriken's actual gravity-affected flight path, fading out over distance. Player can see exactly where it will land. Reticle at arc end. High information, slightly more complex visually.

SLOW

C — Aim Line + Crosshair

Dashed line from player to cursor shows aim direction (not gravity-adjusted). Crosshair at cursor. Fast to read at a glance; player still judges the drop mentally. Middle ground between A and B.
