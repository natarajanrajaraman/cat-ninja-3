Slow-Mo: Screen Effect

While right-click is held, what visual cue signals that slow-mo is active?

none

A — No overlay

Only the crosshair changes. The world just moves slower — no screen tint or border. Minimal, keeps art readable.

SLOW-MO

B — Blue vignette + border

Soft blue radial vignette darkens the edges; a blue border frames the screen. Clearly communicates the mode shift. Classic "focus" look.

focus

C — Subtle desaturate

World slightly washes out; crosshair stays vivid red. Draws eye to the cursor. Understated, works with any art style without being distracting.
